Try the probes:
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward deploy/{{ include "titanium.fullname" . }} {{ .Values.admin.port }}:{{ .Values.admin.port }}

Edit `config` in your values and `helm upgrade`: the ConfigMap changes and the pods reload it in place (--watch).

`kubectl rollout restart deploy/{{ include "titanium.fullname" . }}`.
