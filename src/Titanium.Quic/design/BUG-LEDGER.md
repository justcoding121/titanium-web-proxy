## What has run

The in-process network corrupts a datagram and can black-hole every datagram after the handshake. `BytesArriveOnceAndInOrder` sends 256 bytes through delay, reordering, and duplication. `OneCorruptedDatagramIsNotDeliveredAsStreamData` flips one bit of one datagram. `ABlackholeDropsThePayload` sends 32 bytes into a network that drops everything. `AFlippedHandshakeDatagramDoesNotThrow` delivers a bit-flipped Initial and the handshake still completes. An empty datagram is not queued. A DATAGRAM frame closes the connection with protocol violation 0x0a and does not become stream data. Packet and frame seeds already refuse to throw. `MutatedDatagramsDoNotEscapeOrGrowWithoutBound` flips, truncates, inserts, and duplicates a client Initial and a STREAM frame. Nothing throws, and the received stream stays within the mutant. A flipped byte of a sealed stream datagram is discarded and the connection stays open.

On linuxmini, with `TITANIUM_QUIC_PLATFORM_LISTENER=1`, a managed client completes a handshake to a platform listener and both sides exchange 32 bytes. A platform client to the managed listener already exchanges 32 bytes on this PC. The public interop runner, h3spec, Stryker, and the 30 minute soak have not run. One dropped datagram still closes the receiver with flow-control error 0x03 once the next flight passes the 16384-byte reassembly cap. That limit is recorded and is not a new row.
